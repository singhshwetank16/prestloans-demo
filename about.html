Who We Are

Fintech Agility Powered by 30+ Years of NBFC Heritage

Prest Loans is the flagship digital trade name under which Lord Krishna Financial Services Ltd operates its modern financial services. Incorporated in 1992, our company is registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) as a Non-Deposit taking Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC-ND).

For over three decades, we have remained dedicated to powering the economic backbone of India: small and micro enterprises, manufacturers, wholesale merchants, retailers, and modern commercial EV drivers. By replacing lengthy bank queues with sophisticated credit algorithms and alternative data analytics, we approve and disburse loans within 48 hours.
