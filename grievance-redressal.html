Framework & Core Principles

Customer service is fundamental to our institutional mission. This Grievance Redressal Policy is formulated pursuant to directions issued by the Reserve Bank of India on the Fair Practices Code for Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

Fair & Equitable Treatment

All customers, borrowers, and applicants are treated with utmost fairness, dignity, and transparency at all touchpoints without discrimination.

Courteous & Timely Response

Complaints are acknowledged swiftly and addressed with professional courtesy, empathy, and adherence to strict regulatory turnaround timelines.

Clear Escalation Avenues

Salient Features of the Scheme RB-IOS 2026

What you can complain about

Any “deficiency in service” by us: any shortfall or inadequacy in a service we are required to provide, whether or not it caused you a financial loss.

Complain to us first

You must first complain to Prest Loans, in writing or otherwise. The RBI Ombudsman will not take up a complaint that was not first raised with us.

When you can go to the Ombudsman

  • We haven’t replied within 30 days of receiving your complaint, or
  • We rejected it, or
  • You’re not satisfied with our reply.

Time limit

You must file within 90 days of the date our 30-day period ends, or of our last communication, whichever is later.

How to file

Online through the CMS portal (https://cms.rbi.org.in), by email (crpc@rbi.org.in), or by signed letter to the CRPC (addresses above). Keep ready a copy of your complaint to us, our reply (if any), and supporting documents.

When a complaint won’t be accepted

  • The same grievance is already pending before or decided by the Ombudsman, or
  • Is pending before or decided by a court, tribunal, arbitrator or other forum, or
  • Is time-barred under the Limitation Act, 1963, or
  • Is abusive, frivolous or vexatious, or lacks the required information.

Matters outside the Scheme

  • A lender’s commercial judgment, such as a decision on whether to sanction a loan
  • Disputes between a vendor and a lender, or between two lenders
  • Complaints against management or executives
  • Employer–employee disputes
  • Actions taken to comply with court or statutory orders
  • Matters with a remedy under Section 18 of the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005

Process & Award

  • The Ombudsman first tries to settle the complaint through facilitation, conciliation or mediation.
  • If it isn’t settled, the Ombudsman may pass an Award.
  • You must accept the Award in writing within 30 days, or it lapses.
  • Once you accept it, we must comply within 30 days.

Compensation

  • There is no limit on the amount in dispute that can be awarded.
  • In addition, up to ₹30 lakh for consequential loss.
  • Up to ₹3 lakh for loss of time, expenses, harassment and mental anguish.

Appeal & Representation

Appeal: The Appellate Authority is the Executive Director in charge of RBI’s Consumer Education and Protection Department. You may appeal within 30 days of receiving the Award or rejection; this can be extended once by up to 30 days.

Representation: You may complain yourself or through an authorised representative. The representative cannot be an advocate, unless the advocate is the complainant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding grievance filing, escalation stages, and RBI Ombudsman jurisdiction.

1. What should I do first if I have a complaint regarding my Prest Loans account?

Your first point of contact is our Customer Service Helpline (011-45241131) or email at care@prestloans.com. You can also submit the online form on this page or hand over a written letter at our Corporate Office (Surya Kiran Building, CP) or Registered Office (Asaf Ali Road). You will receive an official acknowledgment and reference number within 2 business days.

2. How much time will it take to resolve my complaint?

Standard service complaints are investigated and resolved within 2 Weeks (14 calendar days) by the Primary Grievance Officer. Matters requiring retrieval of archival records or legal scrutiny are resolved within a maximum period of 2 weeks. If escalated to the Principal Nodal Officer, a response is guaranteed within 7 business days.

3. When can I escalate my grievance to the Compliance Officer or Principal Nodal Officer?

If you do not receive a resolution within 2 weeks of lodging your complaint with the Primary Cell, or if you are dissatisfied with the response, you may escalate to Ms. Sheetal Mishra (Compliance Officer) at compliance@prestloans.com / Tel: 011-45241123. If the issue remains unresolved after 3 weeks, you may escalate directly to Mr. Sandeep Saini (Principal Nodal Officer) at nodalofficer@prestloans.com / Tel: 011-45241131.

4. Under what circumstances can I appeal to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI Ombudsman)?

Under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, if Prest Loans does not respond within 30 calendar days of receiving your complaint, or if the complaint is rejected, or if the resolution provided by the company is unsatisfactory to you, you have the statutory right to file a complaint with the RBI Ombudsman online via the Complaint Management System at https://cms.rbi.org.in or CRPC Portal at https://www.rbi.org.in/, or send by post to Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre (CRPC), 4th Floor, Sector 17, Chandigarh – 160017.

5. Does lodging a grievance affect my credit score or loan status?

No, absolutely not. Filing a grievance or raising inquiries regarding charges, interest calculations, or statements has zero adverse impact on your credit profile. Prest Loans strictly abides by RBI Fair Practices Code; grievances are treated with strict confidentiality, impartiality, and good faith.
