Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, comprehensive answers to all your queries regarding loan eligibility, documentation, repayments, and regulatory compliance.

Is Prest Loans a Reserve Bank of India (RBI) registered NBFC?

Yes. Prest Loans is the digital brand name of Lord Krishna Financial Services Ltd, a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under Certificate of Registration No. B-14.02052. Incorporated and operating with high distinction since 1992, we strictly adhere to all statutory RBI Master Directions, Fair Practices Codes, and digital lending benchmarks.

What is the eligibility criteria for an MSME Business Loan?

Our streamlined eligibility requirements are designed to accommodate thriving enterprises:
• Minimum operational vintage: At least 1 full year in active business
• Annual bank turnover: ₹10 Lakhs or above
• Promoter / Proprietor age: Between 21 and 65 years
• Bureau credit score: CIBIL 650+ preferred (credit history evaluated holistically)
• Active commercial bank account demonstrating consistent monthly transactional velocity.

Can I secure an enterprise business loan without pledging collateral?

Yes, absolutely! Our flagship MSME Working Capital Loans and E-Commerce Vendor Financing are 100% collateral-free. You do not need to mortgage residential, commercial property, or pledge gold assets. Credit decisions are evaluated through algorithmic cash-flow analysis and verified bank statements.

What is the expected turnaround time for loan disbursal?

Prest Loans operates an entirely paperless, end-to-end digital lending pipeline. Following rapid digital KYC verification and bank statement scrutiny, approved funds are credited directly to your registered bank account via NEFT/RTGS within 24 to 48 business hours.

What is the application procedure for EV (Electric Vehicle) Commercial Financing?

We finance up to 85% of on-road vehicle costs for commercial 2-wheelers, 3-wheelers, and electric rickshaws. Select your desired model through our extensive authorized dealership network, submit your Aadhaar, PAN, and address proof for digital verification. We offer highly affordable daily, weekly, or monthly repayment amortization models.

How are scheduled EMI repayments executed?

Prior to disbursement, an automated National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) verified e-NACH / e-Mandate is activated, allowing your monthly installment to be deducted seamlessly on scheduled due dates. Borrowers can also execute advance payments or prepayments anytime via UPI, Debit Card, or Net Banking on our borrower portal.

Are there foreclosure fees or prepayment penalties?

In complete compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulatory directives, floating-rate business and individual term loans carry Zero Prepayment and Zero Foreclosure penalties. Borrowers retain complete freedom to close their loan early without extra cost.

How can I reach customer support or escalate a grievance?
