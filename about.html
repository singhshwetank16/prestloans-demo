Who We Are

Fintech Agility Powered by 30+ Years of NBFC Heritage

Prest Loans is the flagship digital trade name under which Lord Krishna Financial Services Ltd operates its modern financial services. Incorporated in 1992, our company is registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) as a Non-Deposit taking Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC-ND).

For over three decades, we have remained dedicated to powering the economic backbone of India: small and micro enterprises, manufacturers, wholesale merchants, retailers, and service enterprises. By replacing lengthy bank queues with sophisticated credit algorithms and alternative data analytics, we approve and disburse loans within 48 hours.

Ashok Mittal

Founder & Director — Prest Loans (Lord Krishna Financial Services Ltd)
30+ Years Leadership Harvard Business School Exec Alumnus Gold Medallist (MDS) Ex-Union Bank & UCO Bank MBM & CAIIB

Mr. Ashok Mittal is the visionary Founder and Director of digital lending NBFC Prest Loans (Lord Krishna Financial Services Ltd). He is a seasoned financial sector leader possessing over 30 years of rich executive experience spanning Capital and Commodities Markets, Banking Operations, Financial Services, and Foreign Exchange.

Ashok completed the prestigious executive program "Managing and Transforming Professional Service Firms" at Harvard Business School. A gold medallist commerce graduate from M.D.S. University, Rajasthan, he holds a Master’s in Banking Management (MBM) and professional CAIIB credentials. His expansive career features key leadership positions at premier financial institutions, underpinned by foundational commercial banking tenures with Union Bank of India and UCO Bank.

A prominent voice in Indian fintech and credit markets, he has been a regular expert on leading business television networks and print publications for over a decade. He has conducted more than 200 public seminars across capital market segments and addressed elite national conventions hosted by PHDCCI, FICCI, ASSOCHAM, National Stock Exchanges, and ICFAI.

National Felicitations: Ashok has been honored by eminent national dignitaries including former President of India late Mr. Pranab Mukherjee, Prime Minister Mr. Narendra Modi, and Union Home Minister Mr. Amit Shah. In November 2022, he authored and released "The Fintech Entrepreneur's Guide", delivering strategic blueprints for scalable fintech software and visionary credit design.
