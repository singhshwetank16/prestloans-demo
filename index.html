Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, definitive answers regarding our credit appraisal process, eligibility thresholds, documentation requirements, and competitive interest rates.

Is Prest Loans a Reserve Bank of India (RBI) registered NBFC?

Yes. Prest Loans is the digital brand of Lord Krishna Financial Services Ltd, a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) registered with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under Registration No. B-14.02019. Operating with distinction since 1992, we strictly adhere to RBI Master Directions, Fair Practices Code, and digital lending governance.

What documents are required to apply for a loan?

Our application workflow is 100% digital and paperless. Applicants primarily require:
1. Applicant KYC Identification (PAN Card & Aadhaar Card)
2. Last 6 to 12 months bank statements
3. Income proof or salary slips (for salaried) / business continuity proof (for self-employed)
4. Proof of residence / address.

What is the typical turnaround time from application to disbursement?

Upon completing digital KYC verification and e-signing your sanction agreement, funds are directly disbursed to your verified bank account via NEFT/RTGS within 24 to 48 business hours.

How is monthly loan installment (EMI) repayment managed?

Before disbursal, an automated e-NACH / e-Mandate is activated via National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) verified rails, enabling seamless debit on scheduled due dates. Borrowers can also make instant advance repayments anytime through our portal via UPI, Debit Card, or Net Banking.

Can I obtain a personal or education loan without pledging physical collateral?

Yes, absolutely! Our Personal Loans, Education / Coaching Loans, and Short Term Loans are 100% collateral-free. Sanctions are based on automated analysis of your bank cash flow consistency, income track record, and credit score without requiring property or asset mortgages.

Whom can I contact for queries, assistance, or grievance redressal?

You can reach our dedicated corporate helpdesk at 011-45241131, connect via WhatsApp at +91-9711669090, or email us at info@prestloans.com. In accordance with RBI guidelines, an appointed Principal Nodal Grievance Redressal Officer is also available at nodalofficer@prestloans.com for escalation.
