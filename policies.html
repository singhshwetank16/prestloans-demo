1. Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: January 2026

Acceptance of Digital Loan Terms

These Terms and Conditions govern the access and use of the digital lending platform, website, and credit services operated by Lord Krishna Financial Services Ltd under the brand name "Prest Loans". By submitting a loan request, uploading KYC documents, or authorizing an e-NACH mandate, the borrower agrees to these terms.

Borrower Eligibility & Sanction

Loans are sanctioned based on automated and manual underwriting criteria, bank cash flow scrutiny, CIBIL bureau rating, and verified turnover proofs. Sanction and disbursement remain at the sole discretion of the NBFC.

Electronic Repayment Mandate

Borrowers agree to maintain sufficient balance in the designated bank account on installment due dates. Any bounce caused by insufficient funds will incur standard bounce charges as disclosed in the Schedule of Charges.

10. Interest Rate Model and Schedule of Charges

LKFSL Reference Base Rate: 18.00% p.a.

Lord Krishna Financial Services Limited ("Prest Loans") maintains a Reference Base Rate of 18% p.a. Pricing is structured on an objective Discrete Interest Rate Model anchored against institutional cost of funds, operational expenses, tenure matching, and borrower credit risk gradation.

Key Fact Statement (KFS) Transparency

In accordance with RBI Master Directions on Fair Practices Code, all processing charges, documentation expenses, stamp duty, bounce charges, and Annualized Percentage Rates (APR) are explicitly stated in the Key Fact Statement prior to loan agreement signing. Penal charges are strictly non-capitalized under RBI Circular RBI/2023-24/53.
